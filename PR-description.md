# PR Title

feat/FEAPP-420: Add WhatsApp notifications for product updates

## WHAT

Add support for optional WhatsApp notifications for product listing updates. Customers who provide a WhatsApp number will receive notifications through both email and WhatsApp. Existing email notification functionality will remain unchanged.

## WHY

Customers have requested WhatsApp as an additional channel for receiving product update notifications. This enhancement provides more flexibility in how customers receive updates.

## Notes

- WhatsApp notifications are optional.
- Existing email notifications must continue to work as before.
- Customers who provide a WhatsApp number should receive notifications through both channels.
- Test email-only and email-plus-WhatsApp notification scenarios.
